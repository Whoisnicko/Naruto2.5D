## Roadmap
Keep Mist Saga intact west of Konoha. Next Kabuto, then Gaara, then Orochimaru. Independent optional discoveries, no mandatory campaign order, no dynamic level scaling. Each has separate arena, intro/ending RPG dialogue, unique reward once, sandbox launch, independent cooldowns, attack tells, no escape while engaged, isolated world simulation, death retry/checkpoint, support for existing saves and immortal testing. Avoid coupling new victories to legacy two-boss campaign completion. Shared data-driven world boss registry and arena lifecycle first; preserve legacy mistStage/kyubiUnlocked and defeated fields. Add separate validated worldBossProgress record with migration defaults; test save/export/import, no duplicate rewards, old campaign completion, sandbox restoration, geometry and boss cooldowns. Review actual sprites/animations before integration; no mock image presented as final gameplay.
## Kabuto — The Medical Prodigy
Suggested difficulty level 8–10; north-eastern abandoned medical outpost, location provisional pending collision/route checks. HP 1500 provisional. Chakra Scalpel: fast approach, 0.6s tell, 3s cooldown; Healing Jutsu: stationary 2s channel, 20s cooldown, heal 6% max HP (90), interruptible via heavy hit; Medical Disruption: 1s tell, 12s cooldown, chakra recovery reduced by 40% for 5s; Evasive Step: 7s cooldown, dodge sideways, no prolonged invulnerability. At 50% HP shorter movement pauses, alternating feints; no reset to full HP or hidden cooldown reset. Reward medical gloves (+chakra recovery/ninjutsu, exact stats pending). Solo encounter, no required companion. Cap healing per encounter at 18% max HP to prevent endless stalemates.
## Gaara — The Sand Guardian
Suggested level 11–14, wind frontier desert, exact location provisional. HP 1900 initial. Sand barrier: limited absorb shield, 18s cooldown, lasts up to 4s or breaks on damage; Sand Coffin: visible 1.2s ground warning, 14s cooldown; Sand Wave: 8s cooldown; phase 2 at 50% HP partial Shukaku transformation, larger attacks with readable warning. Unique sand armor. No full Shukaku form until separate scope.
## Orochimaru — The Serpent
Suggested level 15–20, hidden underground laboratory, location provisional. HP 2400 initial. Snake summons 18s, Kusanagi sweep 3.5s, lunging snake strike 9s, limited shedding heal 25s, phase 2 at 50% HP. Unique Kusanagi sword. Avoid permanent invulnerability or regeneration loops.
## Balance gate
Proposed automatic level growth still awaiting approval (+5 HP, +3 chakra, +2 attack, +1 defense every 3 earned levels, +0.5% speed, +0.5 percentage points crit; bonus cap 20). Current level only grants skill points; recommended levels and HP are provisional until growth and playtest are validated. New boss mechanics not implemented; start Kabuto only after approval of design/asset direction.

## v1.12b beta — Kabuto: The Forbidden Surgeon
Kabuto retains 1500 HP. Movement speed 4.9; phase 2 +25%; scalpel damage 24, Surgical Cross 27. Medical Regeneration restores up to 450 HP per 2.8-second cast, 20-second cooldown after execution or interruption, starts at 70% HP or lower, 1350 HP cap per attempt. Only a damaging Rasengan/Giant Rasengan or explicitly marked offensive special technique from another playable character interrupts; ordinary attacks, critical hits, guard breaks, clones and Naruto's projectiles do not. Interrupt grants 2 seconds recovery. Phase transition waits for active healing to finish or be interrupted. Disruption 16 damage, 65% reduced chakra regeneration for 8s; new Chakra Extraction (14s cooldown, 1.15s tell, radius 3.3, 15 damage, drains up to 28 chakra on unavoided hit); phase-two Autopsy Protocol (15s cooldown after third pulse, three sequential 0.9s re-aimed ground warnings, 14 damage each). Green cast bar, purple extraction and amber autopsy warnings. Save schema, Mist bosses and production unchanged. Focused logic test and syntax checks only; full regression, browser visuals, balance and Cloudflare deployment pending. Preview https://feature-v112-world-bosses.naruto2-5d.pages.dev/.
