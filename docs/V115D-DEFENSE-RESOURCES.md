# Defense and Resources — v1.15d


## v1.15d beta — Guard, Stamina & Chakra Charge
Always-on player aim assist; enemy melee can retarget even 180 degrees during preparation, with a 140ms lock before impact and directional, obstacle-aware hits. Selected physical boss attacks turn during early preparation; scripted, lunging and multi-pulse attack directions retained. Hold B to block frontal tagged physical attacks; full absorption if stamina covers 8 + 1.15 × raw damage; 100 max stamina, 6/s hold drain, regeneration 22/s after .65s rest, .85s guard break and unblocked damage if insufficient; movement 55% speed while guarding. Dash and special jutsus retain prior costs and rules except guard break prevents action. Hold V while stationary to charge chakra: .25s windup then +24/s over passive regen, interruption on damage, action, movement or release; Kabuto disruption still applies. B/V remappable, pointer hold buttons, old keybindings migrated, HUD stamina and status, procedural guard arc and chakra aura, reuse existing sprites. Stamina transient (full on reload), sandbox restores prior stamina. Full mocked regression suite and new defense/charge/enemy-turn/binding/sandbox checks passed. No real-browser FPS, touch or visual QA yet. Production main remains v1.15b.
